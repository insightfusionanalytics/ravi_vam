================================================================================
  IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
================================================================================

  1. DATA WINDOW: 5.2 years (2020-2025) — mostly bull market. The
     strategy has not been tested through multi-year bears (2000-2003,
     2007-2009). Results may not generalize to all market conditions.

  2. MAX DRAWDOWN: -38% to -39% is significant. A $100K portfolio
     would have dropped to ~$62K at the worst point. SPY's max DD
     over the same period was approximately -25%.

  3. 2022 PERFORMANCE: The strategy lost 29-32% in 2022. The kill
     switch prevented catastrophic losses (UPRO unprotected lost ~65%),
     but the strategy still underperformed SPY (-19%) in the crash year.

  4. KILL SWITCH WHIPSAW: In Mar-Apr 2022, the strategy executed 4
     round trips in 20 days as SPY oscillated around the 200-day SMA.
     This generated ~$700 in unnecessary costs. The kill switch has
     no confirmation period on the 200-SMA leg.

  5. STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE: Alpha of 7-8% over 5.2 years is not
     conclusive. The confidence interval is wide enough that the true
     alpha could be zero. This is historical outperformance, not a
     guarantee.
